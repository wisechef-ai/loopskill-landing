The white space

A vetted registry of safety-bounded loops

People hand-roll agentic loops and they spin out — runaway processes, $400 overnight bills. No vetted loop library exists. LoopSkill loops carry their safety envelope before you pull them: max turns, tool allow-list, a verification script, and explicit stopping criteria. Pull one, run it unattended, sleep soundly.

# pr-review-loop · loopskill.io/loops success_condition: "review comment posted" verification: ./verify.sh max_turns: 10 budget_usd: 2.00 tool_allowlist: - github_read_pr - github_post_comment stopping: success: comment_posted failure: max_turns # ↑ the safety envelope, visible # before you ever run it
